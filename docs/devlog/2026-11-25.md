# Devlog — 2026-11-25 — Depth update

Feedback: "for some reason I can't name, it feels monotonous". The diagnosis: every city played the same way (open all four sources, read, fly), with no decisions, no surprises and nothing at stake between cases. Eight of the ten proposals were approved; this update builds them ([ADR-0015](../decisions/0015-depth-update.md)). Points 8 and 10 stay for later.

## What changed
1. **Choose what to investigate.** Two investigations per city before the trail goes cold; each one after that costs 3 h more. Every source shows how clear its clue is (clear / medium / vague), so each visit is a trade between hours and certainty.
2. **Surprise events.** Ten cards (informant, strike, corrupt cop, ambush, envelope, witness, pickpocket, storm, colleague, reporter). Each offers two choices; some lead into a mini-game, then a "What you got" card shows the reward.
3. **A thief who reacts.** A lead strip shows how many hours ahead they are, plus an intercepted message (far / close / here / you missed / planted). Get close from case 3 and they react: tip-off, burned source, false lead, or thugs at the exit.
4. **Gang tricks.** Each gang has a twist, warned about in a gang file at the briefing: forged clues (Paper Saints), sea legs without flight data (Blue Harbour), jammed wiretaps that need decrypting (Zero Signal), body doubles in CCTV (Velvet Hands), all of them (Glass Serpent).
5. **Gear.** Eight gadgets unlocked with stars, three slots per case, each the answer to a gang trick (lens → doubles, decoder → jams, radar → sea legs, lie detector → forgeries…).
6. **Objectives.** *Recover the loot*: the money trail describes the hideout; raid one of six on the map. *Rescue*: escape chase with the hostage. *Split*: two thieves part ways; follow either branch.
7. **Combined clues.** Later cases split a fact in two halves; two sources together point to one country.
8. **City as a map.** Each city is a drawn district map (streets, coast or river, landmark) with the sources and the airport as pins.
9. **Story.** Short scenes before and after each case; the arc ends with Dr. Varela revealed as the Collector.

## Balancing
Time budgets are now computed from the route: par per city (cost of the clearest source + travel) × a slack per gang, plus room for a number of worst-case mistakes. A greedy "investigate everything" bot must win the easy cases and lose from case 12 on. Two worst-case wrong flights still leave 55 of 60 runs winnable.

## Visual review: what it caught
All 23 new situations rendered in desktop, phone and landscape (`tests/depth_tour.gd`) and checked one by one:
- **Raid map labels overlapped** (A and E in Cairo). Pin separation was measured in 0–1 map coordinates, which ignores the map's shape and the label widths. Pins are now laid out in pixels: each takes the nearest free spot (search in growing rings), with two more strategies before giving up; if the names still don't fit (long Spanish names on a small phone), the map shows only letters, since the list names every place. The landmark is placed first when possible. The layout is cached per city and size.
- New **`tests/city_map_check.gd`**: every city, both languages, 9 map sizes, sources and hideouts. Nothing may overlap, stick out or sit in the water, and wide maps must show names and the landmark. 1,764 maps, 58,550 checks, now in CI.
- **A tip followed the player** to the next screen (the "lead" tip appeared on the travel screen). An unread tip is now dropped when the screen changes, without marking it as read, so it returns next time.
- **Story lines skipped while typing:** the first tap now finishes the line; the second moves on.
- **The violinist hostage** got a random portrait (hat, short hair). The story cast now has fixed looks, checked by the data validator.
- **Landscape header:** "Hours: 29 / 130" wrapped onto two lines; the clock no longer wraps.

## Verification
- Logic: 15,923 checks (bots win every case and branch with and without gear; every event option is safe; hideouts and combined clues are solvable; plans are deterministic).
- UI flows (every case, branch and escape through the real UI): 7,151 desktop / 8,864 phone / 8,864 landscape, including the new tip and story checks.
- Tutorial: 223 checks in each of the 3 layouts. City maps: 58,550. Data validator + 13 Python tests.

Fugitive mode was not changed in this update.
