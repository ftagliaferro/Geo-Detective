## Core twist — "too close to the classics" (same day)
Playtest feedback: the slice felt too similar to the classic geography-chase formula. Changes (ADR-0006):
- **Pin-drop pursuit**: no list of destinations. A 3D globe (Natural Earth borders rendered by a Python tool into a neon texture) — drag to spin, tap to drop a pin. Within the case radius you stay on the trail; otherwise you land in the nearest of 42 world cities and lose time. Precision is scored.
- **Forensic evidence**: wiretap transcripts, money trail (receipts, crypto) and CCTV street footage replace witnesses/informants.
- **Corkboard**: suspect cards you cross out by hand; the last one standing gets the arrest order.
- **Arcade chase**: low-poly 3-lane runner (keyboard, swipe/drag or tap) — catching the suspect earns a star.
- Tests: 38 GDScript (incl. pin resolution, lat/lon round trip, chase simulation) + 5 Python. End-to-end playthrough verified in Chromium (globe pick lands on Portugal at 38.5°N 8.5°W).
- Street photos: curated sources chosen on Wikimedia Commons; download tool records author/license into `CREDITS.md`. Download pending while network access to Commons stabilises.

## Visual pass + chase feel (same day)
Feedback: "the chase is hard and the arrow keys don't respond well; graphics aren't pretty yet".
- Chase controls are now event-driven (every key press counts, no focus stealing), with on-screen < > buttons for touch; lane switch is snappier.
- Difficulty retuned: 30 s, smaller start gap, smaller hit penalty, fewer barriers early (max one per row in the first 8 s, always a free lane).
- (Superseded by the classic restyle below.) Synthwave look: procedural night sky, striped retro sun, scrolling neon grid, shader-lit building windows, low-poly cars with tail lights, glow.
- UI: animated background shader with perspective grid, subtle CRT scanlines, Bebas Neue titles and Space Mono "typewriter" evidence text (both OFL), spinning globe on the title screen, 3D map pin.
- Final art (character portraits, music, real street photos) remains planned for Phase 4.

## Restyle: classic instead of sci-fi (same day)
Feedback: "too futuristic — use normal colours, the interface must not look like science fiction".
- Palette switched to a detective-dossier look: charcoal panels, paper, stamp red and brass accents; neon grid background and CRT scanlines removed.
- Globe texture regenerated as a vintage atlas (muted blue ocean, parchment land, sepia borders).
- Chase is now a city street at dusk: asphalt, white lane markings, pavements, brick/stone buildings, navy unmarked car chasing a dark red getaway car, real shadows.


## From demo to full campaign (same day)
Feedback: "make it a complete, deeper game — more and longer cases, harder clues, more graphics; keep the demo".
- Demo preserved on branch `demo-v0.1`.
- 15 cases in 5 gangs, routes of 3–8 cities; 39 cities with three tiers of wiretap clues and CCTV hints (all written in ES/EN).
- Content pipeline: `campaign_content.py` (writing) → `generate_campaign.py` (seeded search for fair suspect traits, time budgets from perfect runs).
- Difficulty: clue tier, pin margin (1000 → 350 km), paid CCTV analysis, tighter time, bigger gangs.
- Case select with locks and stars, saved progress, six ranks with promotions.
- Graphics: animated flight arc on the globe, procedural mugshot portraits that show visible traits (glasses, scarf, scar, ring, tattoo).
- Street photos for Lisbon and Budapest (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0 / CC0) after allowing `thumb.wikimedia.org`.
- Tests: perfect playthrough of all 15 cases, solvability of every case, tiers, CCTV analysis, progress persistence (83 GDScript + 6 Python).

## Fugitive mode — play the thief (same day)
Request: "split it in two, cop or robber — design it yourself".
- Designed a pursuit model: lead = hours until Inspector Rivas reaches your city; actions spend lead, leaving cities gains it (she investigates), detours cost (she flies straight).
- Thief toolkit: boss's messages, smuggler's map, clean tracks, false leads. Escape chase when you arrive with < 10 h lead.
- 5 escapes (4–8 cities), balanced by simulation of four play styles.
- `escape_state.gd` + 11 new tests; shared shortlist/tier helpers moved to `geo.gd`; chase escape mode with patrol car and siren.
- New CI step: `tests/smoke_ui.gd` opens every screen of both modes headless and fails on any script error.
- 236 GDScript tests + UI smoke + 7 Python tests.
