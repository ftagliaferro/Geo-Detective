# ADR-0004: Theme & tone — international organized crime

- **Status:** Accepted — 2026-11-14

## Decision
The player is an agent of an original international task force hunting organized-crime networks. Each case features a different crime family, so the roster is varied:
- Drug cartels / smuggling routes
- Con artists & identity fraudsters (forged checks, fake pilots/doctors — "catch me if you can" vibe)
- Cyber-criminals & crypto scams
- Art & antiquities traffickers
- Counterfeiters & money launderers

## Tone guardrails
Gritty crime-thriller *atmosphere* (noir UI, dossiers, wiretaps, mugshots), but **no graphic violence, no drug-use depiction, no instructions** of any real crime. Arrests, not killings. Villains and organizations are fictional; no real people, gangs or brands.

## Consequences
- Expected store ratings: ESRB **Teen** / PEGI **12–16** / Google Play **Teen** (references to drugs & crime). This keeps the game sellable and compatible with standard AdMob ads.
