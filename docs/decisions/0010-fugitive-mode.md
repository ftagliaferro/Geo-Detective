# ADR-0010: Fugitive mode — play the thief

- **Status:** Accepted — 2026-11-14

## Context
Request: "split it in two: you can be the cop or the robber — add a mode where you are the one escaping". The owner left the design to us.

## Design goals
1. Same geography core (decode clues → drop a pin on the globe), seen from the other side.
2. A different tension: not a fixed clock but a **pursuer** whose distance depends on your choices.
3. Decisions with trade-offs, not "buy every clue".

## Rules
- You start at the heist city at t = 0; **Inspector Rivas** (AI) reaches it after a *head start*.
- Your **lead** = hours until she reaches the city you are in. Every action spends hours (lead shrinks); every city you leave on the real trail makes her stop and investigate (*inspector_hours*), so leaving gains lead.
- Thief actions per city: boss's message (1 h), second message (2 h), smuggler's map with four countries (2 h), **clean tracks** (3 h, +6 h to her investigation there), **false lead** (2 h, +10 h, 2 per escape).
- **Wrong pins hurt:** she does not follow detours — she flies straight from your last real safe house, while you paid for the extra flight.
- Caught when your lead reaches 0. Reaching the last safe house with < 10 h lead triggers an **escape chase** (you drive, a patrol car follows; crashes let it close in; survive 25 s).
- Stars: escape · no wrong flights · arrive with ≥ 12 h lead and no chase.

## Content
Five escapes, one per gang, 4 → 8 cities, clue tiers 1 → 3, pin margin 1000 → 450 km, head start 10 → 7 h, inspector 6 → 4 h per city. Generated by `tools/generate_campaign.py` into `game/data/escapes.json`.

## Balance (simulated)
Reading one message per city escapes every mission with ~25 h lead; buying messages + map ends with 12–19 h; buying everything in the hard escapes gets you caught — choices matter.

## Implementation
`core/escape_state.gd` (engine-agnostic, unit-tested), shared helpers moved to `core/geo.gd`, chase gets an `escape` mode, separate unlock chain, stars count toward the same detective rank. New `tests/smoke_ui.gd` opens every screen of both modes in CI.
